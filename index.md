**2026-01-08 — Greenhouse Minigame: Grid Logic, Tile States, and Constraint-Based Movement

Designed and implemented a Twine-based spatial minigame to explore systems-driven interaction without relying on branching narrative. The project centers on a grid-based environment where movement is governed by tile states, walkability rules, and positional constraints rather than story choices.

Built the underlying grid logic using coordinate math and conditional checks to determine valid movement, visibility, and state changes. Each tile encodes properties such as passability, interaction affordances, and environmental conditions, allowing the system to respond dynamically to player position.

This experiment functioned as a study in constraint as design material—using limits, rules, and spatial logic to shape experience. The resulting interaction emphasizes navigation, orientation, and discovery, reframing Twine as a lightweight engine for procedural systems rather than purely narrative branching.
